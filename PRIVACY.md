# Privacy Policy — Tumblr Likes Calendar

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Tumblr Likes Calendar is a browser extension that adds a heatmap
calendar of your Tumblr likes to your Tumblr Likes page and lets you
jump to the likes from a specific date.

## Data collection

Tumblr Likes Calendar does **not** collect, store on any server,
transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The developer has no
access to any information about you or your Tumblr account.

## How it works

The extension runs entirely inside your own browser, only on
www.tumblr.com. To build the calendar it reads your own likes through
Tumblr's built-in in-page helper (window.tumblr.apiFetch), using the
session you are already logged in with. It uses no API key, no login
of its own, and no external or third-party server.

## Local storage

The data needed to draw the calendar (such as the dates of your likes,
and, in later versions, tags and image links) is cached **locally in
your browser** (via localStorage / IndexedDB) so the calendar opens
quickly. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you clear
your browser data or remove the extension.

## Permissions

The extension requests access to www.tumblr.com only. This is required
solely to run on your Tumblr Likes page and read your own likes. No
other sites are accessed.

## Third parties

No data is shared with any third party. No analytics, tracking, or
advertising is used.
